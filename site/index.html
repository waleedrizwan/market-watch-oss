How this works

Store prices usually follow raw-material prices with a delay: chips to computers, cattle to beef, crude to gas. For each pair, the builder looks at up to 20 years of monthly history and measures how much of an upstream move reaches the store price and how many months it takes. Upstream moves that have already happened, but haven’t reached stores yet, become the forecast for the next few months, assuming upstream stays where it is now.

Each forecast is also checked against the past: the model is refit using only data available at the time and scored on what stores actually did next. Pairs where that check fails are listed as “no reliable signal” instead of getting a number. Prices are US averages in USD. This is a heads-up, not a guarantee, and not investment advice.
