Market Watch

Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Market Watch is a personal, non-commercial dashboard of public market prices. It doesn’t track you, doesn’t run analytics or ads, and doesn’t sell or share anything about you.

What this site collects from you

Nothing beyond what’s needed to serve the page and check that you’re allowed to see it:

  • No analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or tracking pixels.
  • No requests to other websites. Fonts, scripts and charts are served from this site, so your browser never contacts a CDN, Google Fonts or a data provider while you use it.
  • One preference is kept in your browser’s local storage: the product group you last filtered by. It never leaves your device, and clearing your site data removes it.

Signing in

The dashboard is private to its owner and protected by Cloudflare Access. When you sign in, Cloudflare handles the login (for example, emailing you a one-time code) and sets a CF_Authorization cookie so you stay signed in. That cookie is strictly necessary for access control and isn’t used for tracking.

The site receives your email address inside a signed token, only to confirm you’re allowed in. It isn’t stored or logged by this site.

Hosting and logs

The site runs on Cloudflare Workers. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver and protect the site. Short-lived error and request logs are kept by Cloudflare to help debug the service and are deleted automatically. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Data the site does collect

Once a day, a scheduled job fetches public prices from FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Yahoo Finance and DRAMeXchange, and keeps them in a private repository. These are market prices, not personal data. Links to those sources follow their own privacy policies.

Children

The site isn’t directed at children and doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the source repository, where every revision is visible. For questions, open an issue on that repository.
